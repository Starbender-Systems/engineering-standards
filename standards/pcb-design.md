# PCB design

<!-- authority: standard.pcb-design -->

## Purpose and scope

Board manufacturability, assembly, and physical design constraints.

## Rules

- MUST derive stackup, clearances, drills, copper, and assembly constraints from the selected fabrication process and document their source.
- MUST review component orientation, pin mapping, mechanical fit, and critical routing against the design intent.
- MUST keep fabrication and assembly outputs traceable to one reviewed source revision.
- MUST document unresolved manufacturability or electrical issues before requesting fabrication.
